# Joker pulse plates

The files in this folder (`joker-pulse.webm` and `joker-pulse-sheet.png`) are generated by `scripts/render-joker-pulse.mjs`.

Chromium plays `joker-pulse.webm` (VP9, `alpha_mode=1`). Safari needs HEVC with an alpha plane (`hvc1`). `libx265` on this toolchain accepts `yuva420p` and writes an opaque `yuv420p` file, which iPhone Safari paints as a flat green rectangle, so no `.mov` is shipped. Safari plays the PNG sprite sheet instead. If that sheet is not transparent, or the WebM alpha check fails, the in-code radioactive pulse stays on. A video is never shown until a corner pixel of a decoded frame is transparent.

They are procedural. No stock footage, no third-party textures, and no paid or licensed assets.

The generator output is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
